# PS-10: a fixed 10-diagnosis propensity score (TRACE-ECG version 1 grid)

Written 2026-10-08. This follows the user's decision of 2026-10-08: one fixed list of 10 diagnoses for every trial, plus demographics, and no obesity.

- Code: `scripts/v1_ps10/ps10.py`. It imports `scripts/v21/paper2_grid.py`, `scripts/v20/sens_primary32.py` and `scripts/v18/v18_clmbr.py` and does not change them.
- Outputs (restricted folder, umask 077): `/mnt/raid0/rbc58/ecg-tte/audits/claude-v1-ps10/`.
- This document reports aggregates only: trial counts, means, CIs and P values. It contains no patient count of 1 to 10.

## Plain-language summary

**Does the ECG still help at PS-10? A little, and less than at any other claims-type rung.**
- Adding the ECG embedding (32 PCs) to PS-10 lowers mean |SMD| by **5.0%** on the 54 non-coded items (95% CI −0.8 to 10.3; 23/32 trials better; one-sided P = .051). On the 58-item panel it is 4.9% (−0.6 to 10.1; P = .048). These are paper 1's 32 trials (S32), main configuration.
- On all 38 trials it is 4.8% (−0.5 to 9.8; 27/38; P = .042).
- The CI includes 0 in the main configuration. In three of the five other configurations the CI excludes 0: 1:3 matching 5.2% (1.0 to 9.5), IPTW 3.6% (0.8 to 7.0), overlap weights 6.9% (3.8 to 10.6). With 0.1-caliper matching it is 5.8% (−0.0 to 11.3; P = .029), and with the GBM PS 2.4% (P = .12).
- **The permuted ECG does nothing:** −1.1% (−5.8 to 3.4; P = .64). Against the permuted ECG, the real ECG gives 6.1% (0.0 to 11.0; P = .020).
- **Which domains:** the ECG's gain is concentrated in *vitals and core labs* (8.3%, 1.0 to 15.0; P = .020). The echo domains move by 5–6% (LV structure, LV function, diastolic/LA, valves/aorta), but none is significant on its own.
- **How it compares:** PS-10 already removes part of the imbalance the ECG removed at PS-CVD5. PS-10 alone is 6.8% better balanced than PS-CVD5 (2.4 to 11.2). The ECG's added gain therefore shrinks along the ladder:
  - 11.3% at PS-Demo;
  - 8.8% at PS-CVD5;
  - 5.0% at PS-10;
  - 7.6% at hdPS (which balances slightly better than PS-10 at base);
  - −1.3% at PS-Clinical.
- **RCT agreement:** with the ECG, |Δ log HR| falls from 0.208 to 0.171 (23/32 closer; P = .008). The benchmark-permutation P is .003. Unlike at PS-CVD5, this agreement gain is specific to the true benchmarks in the main, cal01 and 1:3 configurations. It is not specific under IPTW, overlap weights or the GBM PS.
- **CLMBR-T** helps more than the ECG at PS-10: 7.4% (1.8 to 12.6; P = .009), and 9.9% against permuted CLMBR-T. Both together give 7.0% (0.8 to 13.2). The ECG adds nothing on top of CLMBR-T (−0.2%).
- **Leakage:** the only held-out item that reaches the masking threshold is the coded-record composite `B_mean`. It is masked for the PS-10 rung. No physiology item is masked. Five soft proxies (|ρ| 0.50–0.64) are reported. Valve disease is only weakly related to valve severity grades (|ρ| ≤ 0.35).
- **Gates:** all pass, with exact reproduction (max deviation 0).

**Columns 1–8 are PS-CVD5 exactly.** They come from `v18_clmbr.designs(T, n, P5DX)`, called unchanged.

**Columns 9–13 use the same rule as hypertension, CAD and AF.** That rule is `build_core_baseline.build`:
- OMOP gold `condition_occurrence`;
- `condition_source_value` uppercased with dots removed;
- ICD-10 prefix match;
- `condition_start_date` in [index−365, index−1], so the index day is excluded.

Four of the five (CKD, COPD/asthma, PAD, valve disease) and `stroke_history` are already stored in every trial's v1.1 baseline, so they are read from `T.cov`. They are binary and never imputed: the completed values equal the observed values, which the script asserts for every trial.

**TIA (G45) needed re-extraction.** It is stored only in the 10 trials that declare it in `trial_specs` `extra_dx`: active-w, affirm, aristotle, cabana-v2, east-afnet4, frail-af, laaos3, protect-af, rely and rocket-af. `ps10.py extract` re-extracts G45 for all 38 trials with the identical SQL, using the roster from each baseline's `summary.json`.

**Two source differences are inherited from PS-CVD5 and kept as they are:**
- `t2d` and `hf_any_365` (covars v1/covars2b) search `condition_occurrence` plus `observation`. The v1.1 flags search `condition_occurrence` only. All windows are 365 days.
- HF falls back to Elixhauser CHF (`elx_chf`) in 6 trials: life, allhat, ontarget, value, ascot and insight. This is the s10 rule, used because hf_any_365 was dropped there for prevalence under 1%.

**Constant flags.**
- None of the 10 diagnosis flags is constant within any trial, except AF in AMPLIFY (prevalence 0, an exclusion criterion). It is kept, as in PS-CVD5; the L2 PS and the standardisation handle it.
- AF is not constant in the AF trials: 365-day prevalence is 0.67–0.95.
- Prevalence per trial and flag: `flags/design_log.csv` (suppressed where a count or its complement is 1–10).

## 3. Leakage check (before any balance result)

**Method.** This is R2's R4 algorithm (`git show r2:scripts/r2/leakage_map.py`), re-implemented and gated above.
- For each held-out component (`v16_engine.var_components`, all 58 items) and each PS-10 input, compute a pooled Spearman ρ over the stacked rows of the trials where both exist.
- Binary inputs are correlated against the ranks of the held-out component.
- Pairs with fewer than 100 rows, or with a constant column, are not estimable.
- An item is masked if any of its components has pooled |ρ| ≥ 0.80 with any PS-10 input. Pairs with |ρ| ≥ 0.50 are reported as soft proxies and not masked.
- Coverage: 22,614 pairs estimable, 54 not.
- Files: `leakage/pooled.csv`, `items.csv`, `soft_pairs.csv`.

**Masked (|ρ| ≥ 0.80): `B_mean` only.**
- Its pool-B components are the same diagnosis codes as the PS inputs. Examples: dx_I48 vs AF ρ 1.00; dx_E11 vs t2d 1.00; dx_N18 vs CKD 1.00; dx_I50 vs HF 1.00; dx_I25 vs CAD 0.93; dx_I73 vs PAD 0.93; dx_I10 vs hypertension 0.88; dx_I63 vs stroke/TIA 0.81.
- `B_mean` is therefore placed in the PS-10 cells' `ex58`, so it is excluded from every PS-10 endpoint.
- PS-CVD5's v1 cells do not mask it, although its own inputs (AF, T2D, HF) trigger the same rule. In paired PS-10 vs PS-CVD5 contrasts, the item drops from both arms.
- This changes only the 58-item numbers. No non-coded (54-item) item is masked.

**Soft proxies (0.50 ≤ |ρ| < 0.80, not masked).** Pooled ρ, with the within-trial maximum in brackets:

| Held-out item | PS-10 input | Pooled ρ (within-trial max \|ρ\|) |
|---|---|---|
| HbA1c (`pp_LAB__hba1c`) | t2d (PS-CVD5 input) | 0.64 (0.69) |
| eGFR (`pp_LAB__egfr`) | CKD | −0.61 (0.69) |
| observed creatinine (`smd_obs_creatinine`) | CKD | 0.59 (0.67) |
| aortic root (`pp_AORTA__ao_root`) | male (PS-Demo input) | 0.55 (0.59) |
| prognostic score (`smd_prog_full`) | HF | 0.52 (0.66) |

**Pairs the user asked about:**
- **Valve disease** vs AV mean gradient 0.35; vs MR grade 0.33; vs AS grade 0.30.
- **HF** vs LVEF (echo EF) 0.36; vs LV systolic grade 0.38 (elx_chf); vs GLS 0.42; vs LVESVi 0.36.
- **CKD** vs BUN 0.47.
- **COPD/asthma, PAD and stroke/TIA:** no |ρ| above 0.13 with any non-coded item. The largest are PAD vs hemoglobin −0.12 and COPD/asthma vs RVSP 0.12.

So PS-10's new diagnoses are soft proxies for kidney function (eGFR, creatinine). They are not proxies for echo physiology.

**Masks used in the summaries.** A cell's mask is its rung mask plus its arm mask; placebos take the real arm's mask.

## 4. Run

- **Grid:** rung `PS10` × arms base, + ECG (32 PCs), + permuted ECG, + CLMBR-T (64 PCs), + ECG + CLMBR-T, + permuted CLMBR-T × configurations main, cal01, m13, iptw, overlap, gbm × 38 trials. That is 1,368 cells.
- **Code path:** `E.run_cell` exactly as `paper2_grid.task` (L2 C = 1; same matching, Cox and SMD code; `cstat=None`).
- **F1 rule:** cells with fewer than 11 matched pairs, or a failed fit, are treated as failed for every rung in these summaries. No PS-10 cell failed and none has fewer than 11 pairs. The rule removes one trial (PROTECT AF) from 4 hdPS + CLMBR-T cells, and no ladder row used below.
- **Restricted cells** use the same 79-column layout as `claude-v21-paper2/core/restricted_cells.parquet`, with `rung = "PS10"`, so the balance explorer can ingest them:
  - `cells/restricted_cells.parquet`;
  - `cells/cells.csv` (suppressed);
  - `cells/restricted_cells_P5gate.parquet` (the rebuilt PS-CVD5 cells).

**Statistics are paper2's:**
- relative reduction in mean |SMD| over trials, with a 4,000-resample trial bootstrap (seed 20261001);
- items paired within trial;
- exact sign-flip P, one-sided (two-sided for placebos and head-to-heads);
- |Δ log HR| vs the RCT, with a sign-flip P and a benchmark-permutation P (20,000 draws);
- BH q within the PS-10 within-rung family, per set.

Full output: `summary/contrasts.csv`, `domains.csv`, `levels.csv` and `tables.md`.

## 6. Interpretation and caveats

1. **The fixed 10 diagnoses take over part of what the ECG did at PS-CVD5.** The five added diagnoses improve base balance by about 7% over PS-CVD5. The ECG's added gain falls from 8.8% to 5.0%, and its CI now touches 0 in the main configuration.
   - The CI excludes 0 under 1:3 matching, IPTW and overlap weights. Against its permuted placebo the gain is 6.1%, with a CI lower bound of 0.0 (P = .020).
   - What it still balances is mostly vitals and core labs.
2. **PS-10 at base is not distinguishable from hdPS** (−2.3%, CI −10.0 to 4.4). Yet the ECG adds less on top of PS-10 (5.0%) than on top of hdPS (7.6%). The ladder is therefore not monotone in base balance; the overlap in content between the ECG and CKD/HF/valve codes may matter more than the number of codes.
3. **The agreement result is new.** At PS-10 the ECG's |Δ log HR| reduction is benchmark-specific (permutation P .003–.009 in main/cal01/m13). At PS-CVD5 it was not (paper2: permutation P .70).
   - This is one rung and was not pre-specified, so it is exploratory.
   - Pure shrinkage toward the null explained most PS-CVD5 agreement gains (DEFECT_IMPACT §5); that check was not run for PS-10.
4. **Soft proxies:** the CKD flag carries kidney-function information (eGFR −0.61, creatinine 0.59), and t2d carries HbA1c information (0.64, already in PS-CVD5). By the R4 rule these stay unmasked. Readers should know that the "vitals & core labs" and "other labs" balance under PS-10 is partly diagnosis-coded kidney function.
5. **Not done here:**
   - the CV-tuned penalty, which shrank the ECG gain at PS-CVD5;
   - the no-index-day-ECG sensitivity;
   - PS-Clinical without LVEF in the ladder (separate run);
   - the R2 blind 35-trial set.
