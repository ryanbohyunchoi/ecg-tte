# Independent audit: ecg-tte v1.3 / v1.4 exploratory programs (2026-09-25)

Scope: repo `/home/rbc58/github/ecg-tte` at HEAD `ae345f1`, with aggregate outputs in `/mnt/raid0/rbc58/ecg-tte/audits/claude-v13-*` and `claude-v14*`. The audit was read-only. The only computations were small aggregate re-computations from bootstrap and plasmode CSVs and from baseline arm means. No patient-level data are reproduced here.

Severity scale: **critical** = invalidates a headline conclusion; **major** = could change a stated conclusion or its strength; **minor** = wording, consistency or documentation.

No critical code bug was found. The core estimators (plasmode data-generating process, matching, Cox, IPTW and overlap weights, NCO likelihood, v1.4 dropout) are implemented correctly. The problems are in inference (the bootstrap), in three extension-trial emulations, and in several claims that go further than the evidence.

## MAJOR

### M1. The paired bootstrap is off-centre, and the "significant" real-data claims rest on its percentile CIs. Registered-style tests do not reproduce them.
- **Files:**
  - `scripts/v13_summarize.py:98-141` (`bootstrap`)
  - `scripts/v14_summarize.py:67-87` (`boot_d`)
  - `scripts/v13_bootstrap.py:29-47`, `scripts/v14_run.py:100-111`
- **What.** Each replicate resamples patients with replacement, then refits the PS and greedy caliper matching. The bootstrap is known to be inconsistent for matching estimators (Abadie & Imbens 2008). The statistic is a squared-error difference, so its bootstrap mean also picks up Var(a2*) − Var(a1*) and not only the point difference.
- **Evidence (aggregate):**
  - Per-trial C1 Δlog HR, point vs bootstrap mean:
    - RE-LY: 0.185 vs 0.033
    - CABANA: 0.008 vs 0.157
    - LIFE: −0.074 vs +0.027
    - PLATO: 0.072 vs 0.003
  - The same pattern appears at 75% dropout. For RE-LY the point is −0.281 and the bootstrap mean is +0.070.
  - In `docs/v13/bootstrap_shd_enc2_cross_combined.csv`, hdPS200+ENC2 vs R has point 0.0259 with a "95% CI" of −0.016 to 0.015, so the point lies outside its own interval.
  - Several cross-trial points sit at a CI edge. Examples: sparse+noise32 vs RCT is 0.0191 with CI −0.0218 to 0.0191; C1 vs RCT is −0.0009 with CI −0.044 to 0.005.
- **Exact sign-flip tests on the full-cohort point estimates** (the test v1.3 registered as I7) for the claims the report bolds:

  | Claim | Sign-flip result | Trials with ECG closer |
  |---|---|---|
  | Physiology trials, C1 vs RCT (report.md:347, "−0.007 (−0.079 to −0.002)") | p = 0.086 | 6/8 |
  | Dropout 50%, C1 vs RCT | p = 0.15 | 14/18 |
  | Dropout 75%, C1 vs RCT | p = 0.01 | 14/18 |
  | Dropout 90%, C1 vs RCT | p = 0.29 | 9/18 |
  | Dropout 50% / 75% / 90%, C1 vs R+ | p = 0.52 / 0.33 / 0.24 | 12, 15, 9 of 18 |

- **Effect on conclusions.** report.md:439-441 and 451 say that under dropout "sparse + ECG then moves significantly closer to both the RCTs and R+". This is supported only by the percentile bootstrap. It is not supported vs R+ by a permutation test at any dropout level. At 90% deletion only half the trials move closer.
- **Fix:**
  - Report the sign-flip p-value, the count of trials closer and leave-one-out alongside every bootstrap CI.
  - Label percentile-bootstrap CIs for matched estimators as approximate. Consider a subsampling (m-out-of-n) or wild or weighted bootstrap, or a bias-corrected interval centred on the point.
  - Re-word "significantly closer" in report §5c and in the HTML notes (`scripts/build_presentation.py:30`, `presentation_template.html:487`).

### M2. One trial (CABANA) drives the v1.4 real-data dropout result.
- **Files:** `scripts/v14_summarize.py:67-87`; report.md:439-451.
- **Evidence.** Point squared-error contributions, C1 vs RCT, at 75% dropout:
  - CABANA contributes −0.612 of the 18-trial sum. The mean is −0.056, and CABANA alone accounts for −0.034 of it (about 60%).
  - Leave-one-out maximum: −0.023 vs RCT and −0.004 vs R+ (the maximum comes from dropping CABANA).
  - The same holds at 50% and 90%.
  - CABANA is also the most confounded emulation: the unadjusted pp/primary log HR is about −2 and sparse is about −0.9, vs the RCT's −0.15 (see M4).
- **Fix.** Report the leave-one-out range and the result without CABANA. Consider |Δ| (as the multiverse uses) or a robust summary, so that a single grossly confounded trial does not dominate a squared-error mean.

### M3. The EMPEROR-Preserved emulation does not restrict to type 2 diabetes, yet its comparator (DPP-4i) implies T2D.
- **Files:** `scripts/trial_specs.py:465-471` (`gate={"any_before_or_on_index": ["I50"]}`, no E11 requirement).
- **Evidence (arm means, imputation 1):**
  - Coded diabetes: 50.7% on SGLT2i vs 81.2% on DPP-4i.
  - Mean index year: 2022.2 vs 2018.4. Post-2021/22 SGLT2i use in HFpEF is largely in non-diabetic patients, so calendar time is also confounded.
  - Non-diabetic SGLT2i users have no counterpart in the DPP-4i arm. This is a structural positivity problem. It makes "DPP-4i as a placebo proxy" invalid for about half the treated arm.
  - EMPA-REG, by contrast, requires E11 (`trial_specs.py:552-554`).
- **Fix.** Add `"require_all": [["E11"]]` (or an E11 gate) and rebuild. Alternatively, drop EMPEROR-Preserved from the benchmark set or flag it. Record this as a deviation.

### M4. The CABANA emulation is weakly specified. Its endpoint and exposure definitions produce extreme selection, and it is influential.
- **Files:**
  - `scripts/trial_specs.py:485-493` (arms; `proc_vs_drug`)
  - `scripts/trial_specs.py:625` (outcome)
  - `scripts/build_trial_cohort.py:56-65, 116-121`
- **Endpoint.** "Serious bleeding" is a hospitalisation with K920-K922, I62, R58 or **D62** recorded in any position (R58 is unspecified haemorrhage; D62 is acute post-haemorrhagic anaemia). The real event rate is 38.7% (plasmode meta), against about 9% in the RCT over 4 years.
- **Exposure:**
  - The ablation arm must have had no antiarrhythmic order in the 365 days before ablation (step s2 washout). Most real ablation candidates have failed an antiarrhythmic, so the arm is selected, not representative.
  - The drug arm is antiarrhythmic initiators only.
  - Arm means differ sharply: age 65 vs 72, HF 18.5% vs 40.7%.
  - The unadjusted log HR is about −2.
- **Fix.** Restrict bleeding to a primary-diagnosis major-bleed definition, or report the strict variant as primary for CABANA. Reconsider the ablation-arm washout (allow prior antiarrhythmic use and condition on it). Treat CABANA as a sensitivity trial in the cross-trial squared-error summaries.

### M5. The per-protocol (IPCW, naive) and run-in analyses are invalid for CABANA and EAST-AFNET 4.
- **File:** `scripts/v13_extract.py:132-156`.
- **CABANA (`proc_vs_drug`):**
  - The arm-0 "keywords" are CPT/PCS codes passed to `create_drug_tokens`, so the ablation arm has no "own" orders.
  - Every ablation patient therefore gets a "discontinuation" at index + G. `pp_stop{180,365,730}` equals G for the whole arm, so that arm is artificially censored at G days.
  - The antiarrhythmic arm is never censored for crossover to ablation.
  - `repeat_90` = 0 for all ablation patients, so the run-in drops the arm.
  - Evidence: for CABANA R clinical, pp_ipcw_180 is −1.36 and pp_ipcw_365 is −0.99, vs −0.58 primary.
- **EAST-AFNET 4 (`switch_seq`, add-on design):**
  - The "other arm" drug for the treated (antiarrhythmic) arm is rate control, which treated patients are expected to continue.
  - Continued rate-control orders are therefore counted as protocol deviations (29% "switching" by horizon).
- **Effect.** Both trials enter the 18-trial Part II agreement tables and the report's "one consistent pattern" statement (report.md:386-393).
- **Fix:**
  - Skip on-treatment and run-in analyses for `proc_vs_drug` (as for `procedure`).
  - For switch_seq add-on designs, define deviation as stopping the added drug (or starting it, for comparators), not as continuing the prior class.
  - Recompute the Part II tables.

### M6. A headline number is wrong: "about 20% [bias reduction] in physiology trials".
- **Where:** report.md:400; `scripts/presentation_template.html:280`.
- **Evidence.** From `docs/v13/plasmode_rs_by_trial_combined.csv`, base scenario, physiology trials (n = 8):
  - mean |bias| for sparse is 0.0668;
  - the reduction is 0.0093 (0.0043 to 0.0114).
  - Relative reduction ≈ 14%, not 20%. The overall 8% (7.5%) is correct.
- **Fix.** Change to "about 14%".

## MINOR

1. **C2 plasmode is significant harm, not "no gain".**
   - report.md:343 prints "−0.002 (−0.005 to 0.000); no gain".
   - The CSV CI is −0.0047 to −0.0004, which excludes 0.
   - Suggest "slight increase in bias (CI excludes 0)". The same applies to C2 at bottom line 1 ("does nothing on top of hdPS").
2. **The codes-intact row in §5c disagrees with §5b.**
   - report.md:438 gives +0.004 (0.001 to 0.006); §5b gives (0.002 to 0.006).
   - Both come from the same replicates; the Monte Carlo CIs are just redrawn (v14 lo 0.0014, v13 lo 0.0017).
   - Use one source, or note that the MC CIs are recomputed.
3. **The "200 specifications" count is inflated for C1.**
   - The multiverse fits non-hdPS arms only at seed 0 and then copies them to seeds 1-4 (`v13_multiverse.py:129-130`, `v13_summarize.py:151-158`).
   - C1 therefore has 40 distinct specifications, each counted 5 times. The share (100%) is unchanged, but "every one of the 200 analysis specifications" (report.md:405) overstates the evidence.
   - GBM with cross-fitting is also seed-dependent (`StratifiedKFold(random_state=seed)`), so treating it as seed-independent is not strictly right.
4. **Dropout also deletes hdPS lab-order levels.**
   - `Trial.degraded` masks every `self.lv` column, including `labn_*__once` lab levels (`v13_common.py:176-181`).
   - The protocol and the docstring say labs are "kept as recorded".
   - `pci_index_30d` is not degraded, and utilisation is not either (probably intended).
   - Document this or exclude `labn_` levels from the mask.
5. **Under dropout the R+ target is itself degraded.**
   - R+ is core + X_pp, and core's dx/med flags are deleted (per protocol B).
   - "Closer to R+" under dropout therefore means closer to a degraded reference. State this in §5c, or add an undegraded R+ target.
   - Separately, the bootstrap uses one deletion realisation (seed 0), so its CIs do not include deletion randomness.
6. **Protocol v1.4 E is not implemented as written.** It says prior-year HF hospitalisation "is part of R+". `prior_hf_hosp_365` is extracted (`v14_extract.py:67`) but never used in `v14_run.py`. This is an unlogged deviation.
7. **Plasmode design limitation is not stated.**
   - Outcomes are generated from `X_core` only (`v13_plasmode.py:112-134`; `v14_run.py:34-56`). The clinical PS (R) is therefore the correctly specified model by construction.
   - The ECG can help only as a proxy for X_core covariates (meds/utilisation/physiology missing from sparse). Confounders outside X_core are never simulated.
   - The phys_only result (report.md:418-421) implies the C1 gain comes from proxying medication and utilisation terms. The report says the mechanism is "unresolved"; it could say this.
   - In resampled mode the truth is computed on the full-cohort matched set, not per replicate. This is an acceptable approximation but should be noted.
   - In v1.3, Z is standardised on the full cohort while the Cox fit uses `ok` rows. The baseline offset is negligible: simulated and real event rates agree within about 2 percentage points in all 18 trials.
8. **Entropy balancing: code and docstring differ.** `ebal` drops the covariate with the largest |target − sample mean| (`v13_design.py:86`, `argmax(abs(ts))`). The docstring says it drops the largest *remaining gap after weighting*.
9. **Transport uses medians as means for some trials.**
   - For CABANA, `rct_facts.json` records `age_mean` and `bmi_mean` as MEDIANS; ebal uses them as means.
   - ASCOT and CARES Table-1 values are from secondary sources (flagged in the JSON).
10. **NCO calibration: σ collapses to about 0 in most trial-arm fits** (`nco_systematic_error_*`, e.g. sigma 2e-43). The Δσ sign-flip tests (report.md:376-377) therefore compare mostly zeros. Mention this, or use a pooled prior on σ.
11. **The Part II "one consistent pattern" is selective.**
    - hdPS200+ECG beats R in pp_ipcw_730, pp_ipcw_switch, pp_naive_switch and pp_ipcw_180.
    - It does not beat R in pp_ipcw_365 (0.186 vs 0.174) or pp_naive_365 (0.181 vs 0.176).
    - It already beat hdPS200 in the primary analysis (0.147 vs 0.171).
    - The ranges in the table (0.138–0.149 etc.) are correctly transcribed. The finding also includes the invalid CABANA/EAST pp results (M5).
12. **Dose response is not monotone.** report.md:364 says "the effect grows with the amount of ECG information". In fact 8 and 16 PCs are worse than 0 PCs (+0.005, +0.008) before improving at 32 and 64. Say "improves only at ≥ 32 PCs".
13. **E-values are rounded generously.** "About 1.5 in every arm" covers a range of 1.49–1.69 (unadjusted 1.69, sparse 1.64).
14. **Some role labels are debatable.**
    - EMPA-REG is "control", but its data show clear physiology channelling: HF 34.9% vs 21.0% and LVEF 53.8 vs 60.5 in SGLT2i vs DPP-4i.
    - Role-stratified results (physiology n = 8) are sensitive to this label.
    - The labels were set before outcomes, so keep them, but add a sensitivity analysis.
15. **Minor emulation and endpoint notes:**
    - VALUE's outcome uses `cv_death` (includes stroke death) where the trial used cardiac death.
    - Tokenising "sacubitril/valsartan" could put Entresto users in the ARB arms (ONTARGET, VALUE, ELITE-II). This is mitigated by the ever-I50 exclusion in ONTARGET and VALUE.
    - Any-position I50 coding inflates HF-hospitalisation endpoints: real event rates are 44% for EMPEROR-Preserved and 46% for EAST-AFNET 4, vs about 15-20% in the RCTs. This is a frozen phase-2 convention; the strict variant exists.
16. **HTML meta is stale.** `DATA.meta.commit` = `2ecc085` (16:43), which predates the results commit `859c850`. The embedded numbers still match the current CSVs (see below). Regenerate so the stamp is accurate.

## Checked and fine

- **Plasmode data-generating process:**
  - Weibull inversion `t = (−log u / (λ e^{lp}))^{1/ρ}` is correct.
  - The λ adjustment `exp(−β_T · mean(T))` is correct: lifelines 0.30.3 computes the baseline at `_central_values` (covariate means).
  - Simulated and real event rates agree in all 18 trials.
  - Truth uses counterfactual copies with common random numbers, 20 copies, the same administrative censoring, and each arm's own matched population. This correctly handles non-collapsibility.
  - Resampled mode uses the same bootstrap rows per replicate for every scenario (seed 50,000 + rep), with independent outcome seeds per scenario.
  - The cause-of-death end date is used when the endpoint needs cause (COD_END).
  - Scenario `null` is protected from NaN parsing (`keep_default_na=False`).
- **v1.4 dropout:**
  - Plasmode linear predictors come from the undegraded `T` (`std_lps` built before `degraded`).
  - Truth uses undegraded lp on the degraded arm's matched set.
  - dx and medication flags are deleted in X_dx and X_core (so in R and R+ too), and hdPS levels are deleted per code, all levels together.
  - The ECG and X_pp are untouched.
  - hdPS ranking is refitted on degraded data.
- **v1.4 strata:**
  - Density counts distinct dx/rx/px features > 0 in the pre-index panel.
  - Tertiles are trial-specific.
  - Echo flags exclude LAB/BNP/ACCESS; echo is masked before 2016-07-31 and the strata are restricted to the post-2016 period.
  - Feasibility requires a smaller arm ≥ 100.
  - The echo subcohort requires an LVSTRUCT/LVFUNC/DIAST/RVPULM measure; measures are kept at ≥ 20% coverage and median-filled.
- **v1.4 summarizer:**
  - The 80%-successful-replicate filter is applied.
  - Pool-vs-pool differences use common trials only (`pl_diff`, `bs_diff`).
  - The HF RCT target is skipped.
  - The v1.3 resampled plasmode is used as dropout_0 with identical resample rows.
- **Matching:**
  - The 1:1 path reproduces phase-2 `ps_greedy`.
  - 1:k uses the same caliper width, anchor clusters, control weights 1/m, and drops w = 0 rows consistently for the cluster and entry vectors.
  - IPTW is stabilised and trimmed at 1/99% of the PS; overlap weights are correct.
- **IPCW construction:**
  - Rows are per 90-day interval.
  - The weight for interval k uses P(uncensored) through k−1 (`cumprod/ps`).
  - The numerator model includes treatment and interval; weights are truncated at the 99th percentile.
  - Counting-process Cox uses entry times.
  - Limitation (documented): baseline covariates only.
- **Run-in landmark:** requires both pair members event- and censoring-free with a repeat order by day 90; the clock restarts at 90. There is no future-information leakage beyond the landmark.
- **NCO calibration model** (`fit_syserr`): normal random-effects ML; pooled fallback when fewer than 8 NCOs; NCOs with < 11 events excluded; calibrated SE = √(se² + σ²).
- **Benchmark orientation** (`bench`):
  - The CI is inverted when the orientation differs.
  - The SE uses `ci_level` (0.96 EAST-AFNET 4, 0.9502 EMPA-REG, 0.9547 CAROLINA, 0.957 ELITE II).
  - COMET is correctly inverted (1.205, 1.075–1.351).
- **PUBLISHED values for the 8 extension trials** match `docs/v13/rct_facts.json`, the primary papers and our arm order:

  | Trial | HR (95% CI unless stated) |
  |---|---|
  | EMPEROR-Preserved | 0.79 (0.69–0.90) |
  | EAST-AFNET 4 | 0.79 (96% CI 0.66–0.94) |
  | CABANA | 0.86 (0.65–1.15) |
  | ONTARGET | 1.01 (0.94–1.09) |

  Horizons match the published median follow-up.
- **Builder gates:**
  - `require_all` and `first_dx_within` (first I48 ≤ 365 d before index) are implemented correctly.
  - `other_drugs_365d` and `codes_window` use on/before-index data only.
  - The switch_seq sampling uses no future information.
